Why ChiyaSeeYa?

More than just a tea shop, we're a place where people connect. Built around a love for authentic Nepali tea and street-food culture, our café brings together handcrafted teas, refreshing beverages, delicious momos, flavorful snacks, and a welcoming atmosphere. We created this space for those moments that matter—morning tea rituals, afternoon catch-ups, late-night conversations, and everything in between. Every cup is brewed with care, every dish is prepared fresh, and every guest is welcomed like family. Whether you're here for a quick tea, a plate of momo, or a relaxing hukkah session with friends, we're proud to offer a space where good food and good company come together. Come in, take a seat, and make yourself at home.
